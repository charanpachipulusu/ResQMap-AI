Reporting Guidelines

Help the system assess the incident.

  • Enter the correct location or a nearby landmark.
  • Report people trapped or injured honestly.
  • Estimate the number of people affected if known.
  • Describe damage and immediate needs clearly.
  • Leave coordinates blank if you do not know them.

How Prioritization Works

Decision support, not a final emergency judgment.

Life safety: Reports of people trapped or in immediate danger require urgent attention.

Damage: The reported type and extent of damage contribute to assessment.

People affected: More affected people can increase the need for assistance.

Uncertainty: Unverified reports must be checked by responders.
